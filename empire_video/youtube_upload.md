In 1600, England was a small, damp island with no great army and no gold mines. Three hundred years later, it ruled a quarter of all the land on Earth and one in every five human beings.

How did a tiny island pull it off?

In this documentary we trace the rise and fall of the British Empire: the East India Company that grew its own private army, the Royal Navy that ruled the waves, the conquest of India, the Industrial Revolution, railways and undersea telegraph cables, the strategy of "divide and rule", and the terrible price paid by millions. Then, how two world wars and independence movements brought it all crashing down.
