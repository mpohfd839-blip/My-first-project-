One man. Twenty years of war. Every king in Europe against him.

From a poor officer on the island of Corsica to Emperor of the French, Napoleon Bonaparte redrew the map of Europe, crowned himself in Notre-Dame, crushed empires at Austerlitz… and then lost everything in the snows of Russia and the mud of Waterloo.

This animated documentary tells the full story of the Napoleonic Wars: the French Revolution, Egypt and the Rosetta Stone, Trafalgar, Austerlitz, the Spanish "ulcer", the disastrous invasion of Russia in 1812, the Battle of the Nations at Leipzig, exile on Elba, the Hundred Days and the final defeat at Waterloo.
